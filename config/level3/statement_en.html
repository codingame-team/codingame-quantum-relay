Wood 1 League: Quantum Beam Interference is now active! If two opposing laser beams geometrically cross each other, both are shorted out (0 flow) unless one is OVERCHARGEd!

 The Goal

Route more quantum energy from deep-space Pulsars to your Mothership than your opponent over 100 turns.

 Rules

The game takes place in a rotationally symmetric 1600×900 space sector containing two Motherships and 5 Pulsars. Coordinates follow the standard screen convention: x increases to the right, y increases downwards, and (0, 0) is the top-left corner.

Turn Resolution Order

  1. Drone Movement (MOVE x y): Each drone moves up to 120 units per turn toward (x, y), clamped to the sector bounds.
  2. Laser Relay Links (LINK targetId): After movement, each drone projects one outgoing laser beam up to 480 units to either its Mothership (targetId = -1) or another friendly drone (targetId = droneId). Default bandwidth is 6 energy/turn.
  3. Overcharge (OVERCHARGE targetId): Requires cooldown = 0. The drone holds its current position, links to targetId, and boosts its outgoing beam capacity to 12 energy/turn for this turn (sets cooldown to 2).
  4. Quantum Beam Interference: If a player's active beam segment geometrically intersects an opponent's active beam segment:
    • Normal vs Normal (or Overcharged vs Overcharged): Both beams short out (0 effective bandwidth).
    • Overcharged vs Normal: The normal beam is severed (0 bandwidth), while the Overcharged beam retains 6 bandwidth!
  5. Energy Harvesting & Flow: Any drone within 200 units of a Pulsar that has a valid acyclic relay path to its Mothership routes energy from that Pulsar to the Mothership, constrained by the minimum remaining bandwidth along its relay path. Pulsars regenerate 3 to 5 energy at the end of each turn.

Unexecutable Actions: Syntactically valid actions that cannot be executed (such as linking to an out-of-range, nonexistent, self, or opposing target, or using OVERCHARGE while cooldown > 0) are simply ignored and do not cause defeat.

Victory Conditions
  • You have routed more total energy to your Mothership than your opponent at the end of the game (after 100 turns).
Defeat Conditions
  • You have routed less total energy to your Mothership than your opponent at the end of the game.
  • Your program does not respond within the allotted time or outputs a malformed/unrecognized command.

 Game Input & Output

Initialization Input

Line 1: 3 space-separated integers width, height and leagueLevel, the width and height of the space sector (1600×900) and the current Wood division (3 for Wood 3, 2 for Wood 2, 1 for Wood 1).

Line 2: 4 space-separated integers myBaseX, myBaseY, oppBaseX and oppBaseY, the coordinates of your Mothership and of your opponent's Mothership.

Input for One Game Turn

Line 1: an integer turn, the current turn number (1 to 100).

Next line: an integer pulsarCount, the number of Pulsars in the sector.

Next pulsarCount lines: 4 space-separated integers pulsarId, px, py and energy, the unique identifier, coordinates, and currently stored energy of each Pulsar.

Next line: an integer droneCount, the total number of Relay Drones.

Next droneCount lines: 6 space-separated integers droneId, owner, x, y, linkTarget and cooldown:

  • droneId: unique identifier of the drone.
  • owner: 0 if you own this drone (your drones are always listed first), 1 if owned by your opponent.
  • x, y: current coordinates of the drone.
  • linkTarget: current relay target (-2 for none, -1 for Mothership, or a friendly droneId).
  • cooldown: turns remaining before OVERCHARGE is available again.
Output for One Game Turn

One line per owned drone (in the exact order they appear in the input), containing one of the following commands:

  • MOVE x y to move the drone toward the integer target coordinates (x, y).
  • OVERCHARGE targetId to hold position and project an overcharged beam to targetId.

A MOVE command is optionally followed by a relay link:

  • LINK targetId to project a laser beam to your Mothership (-1) or a friendly drone (droneId).

Examples: MOVE 500 450 LINK -1 or OVERCHARGE -1

You may optionally append MSG text at the end of a line to display a message in the viewer.

Constraints
width = 1600, height = 900
pulsarCount = 5
droneCount = 6
Response time for first turn ≤ 1000 ms
Response time for one turn ≤ 50 ms
Maximum number of turns: 100
