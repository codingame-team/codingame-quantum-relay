read width:int height:int leagueLevel:int
read myBaseX:int myBaseY:int oppBaseX:int oppBaseY:int
gameloop
read turn:int
read pulsarCount:int
loop pulsarCount read pulsarId:int px:int py:int energy:int
read droneCount:int
loop droneCount read droneId:int owner:int x:int y:int linkTarget:int cooldown:int
write join("MOVE", "500", "450", "LINK", "-1")

STATEMENT
Deploy your quantum relay drones across deep space! Route unstable pulsar energy back to your Mothership through laser relay chains while cutting enemy beams.

INPUT
width: Width of the space sector (1600)
height: Height of the space sector (900)
leagueLevel: Current Wood division (3=Wood 3, 2=Wood 2, 1=Wood 1)
myBaseX: X coordinate of your Mothership
myBaseY: Y coordinate of your Mothership
oppBaseX: X coordinate of opponent Mothership
oppBaseY: Y coordinate of opponent Mothership
turn: Current turn number (1 to 100)
pulsarCount: Number of energy pulsars in the sector (5)
pulsarId: Unique ID of the pulsar
px: X coordinate of the pulsar
py: Y coordinate of the pulsar
energy: Current available energy in the pulsar
droneCount: Total number of relay drones (4 in Wood 3, 6 in Wood 2/1)
droneId: Unique ID of the drone
owner: 0 if you own this drone (listed first), 1 if owned by opponent
x: X coordinate of the drone
y: Y coordinate of the drone
linkTarget: Current relay target (-2=none, -1=Mothership, or friendly droneId)
cooldown: Turns remaining before OVERCHARGE is available (always 0 in Wood 3)

OUTPUT
One line per owned drone (in the order your drones were given): [MOVE <x> <y> [LINK <targetId>] | OVERCHARGE <targetId>] [MSG <text>]
